# Executive Update: March 2026

The project has moved from pipeline debugging into a much stronger preliminary-delivery phase. The sentence-extraction and classification backbone now covers the full `2016-2024` window, the AI-patent matching layer has been materially improved, Compustat controls now extend through `2016-2024`, and the project can generate clean standalone regression tables and Word-ready review documents. In practical terms, this means the core data and reporting infrastructure needed for a credible preliminary package is now in place.

The most important methodological improvement in the current phase was the discovery that the earlier merged panel kept only firm-years in which firms were already speaking about AI. That object was still useful for a narrow conditional validation question, but it was too restrictive for timing analysis. We therefore rebuilt the sample as an `ever-speaker annual panel`, which keeps all years from `2016` through `2024` for firms that mention AI at least once during the sample window. This broader panel now supports cleaner lagged, contemporaneous, and forward patent tests on a proper annual scaffold.

The current preliminary evidence remains encouraging but measured. In the narrower conditional validation design, speculative disclosure shows a modest positive relationship with future AI patent incidence, while the actionable effect is more mixed. We are treating that as a useful intermediate signal rather than a final claim. The next empirical gate is to estimate the main-text timing table on the rebuilt ever-speaker panel using AI patent outcomes across multiple horizons. That will tell us whether the strongest alignment is backward-looking, contemporaneous, or forward-looking.

The project is therefore in a better position than it was even a few days ago. The major operational blockers have been addressed, the sample design is now much closer to the comparison paper’s logic, and the remaining work is focused on disciplined delivery: building the next main-text tables on the broader panel, validating them one by one, and tightening the narrative before the next paper package is assembled.
